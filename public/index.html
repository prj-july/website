For June Oven owners · 2nd gen today

Keep your oven working after the cloud goes away.

When June's servers shut down, your oven can no longer be paired, controlled, or show its camera. Project July puts a small replacement server on the oven itself, so it keeps working on your home network. No subscription, and nothing sent over the internet.

How it works

The oven becomes its own cloud

Your oven and phone expect to talk to June's servers. Project July answers in the same way, from inside your home. You set it up once.

  1. 1

    A small server runs on the oven

    We install one small program on the oven. It answers the same requests the June cloud used to, so the oven and phone carry on as before.

  2. 2

    The oven points at itself

    The oven's network settings are changed so its “cloud” address leads back to the oven, not to June's servers.

  3. 3

    Your phone uses the Project July app

    The free Project July app for iPhone and Android connects to the oven's address on your home network, using your home's own security certificate. Home Assistant works too.

  4. 4

    The live camera stays local

    A camera program takes still pictures and streams them to your phone and Home Assistant. No video leaves your home.

  5. 5

    Pairing stays at home

    The oven and phone keep, or re-create, the secure pairing they already had. You can pair as many new phones and tablets as you like, and pairing happens entirely on your home network. Only your phones can control your oven.

The Project July app

Set it up once

The guides and files will be linked here soon, once verification and more testing are complete. Here's what to expect.

Every step on a 3D model of the June oven, including the micro-USB plug. It plays when you scroll to it. Drag to look around, or open it full screen.

Before you begin

  • A micro-USB cable that carries data (not a charge-only cable)
  • A laptop
  • A USB stick of 32 GB or more, to start the laptop from (how to prepare it)
  • A screwdriver
  • About one hour

What setup looks like

  1. Unplug the oven, lay it on its side and take out 7 screws.
  2. Plug in the micro-USB cable.
  3. Start your laptop from the USB stick and choose Install.
  4. Hold the SW2 button while you plug the oven back in, then press Install.

The oven is unplugged while you open it. Once it’s plugged back in, touch only SW2 and the micro-USB cable. Watch it in 3D

Security & privacy

Your kitchen stays in your home

No cloud

The oven and your phone talk only to each other on your home network. Nothing is sent to June, to us, or to anyone else.

The camera never leaves home

Camera pictures are streamed only on your local network, and only while you're cooking unless you open the manual view.

Your keys, your oven

The oven and phone use the same secure pairing as before, so only your phones can control your oven. Pairing a new device happens entirely at home. Per-home certificates are on the way.

Open source

The code and build scripts will be published with the first release. You, or someone you trust, will be able to check exactly what runs on the oven.

Where things stand

Project July is an early community project. It isn't made or supported by June or Weber. Setup is a one-time job: seven screws, one micro-USB cable and one button. Per-home certificates are still being finished. Downloads and source code will follow once testing is complete. We'll keep the status list above up to date.

Questions

Frequently asked

Which June ovens does it work with?

The 2nd-gen June Oven is tested and working. The 1st gen should work but hasn't been tested yet. If you have one and would like to help test, get in touch. The 3rd gen is blocked by hardware limits. See the compatibility list.

Do I need to buy a server or leave a computer on?

No. The server runs on the oven itself. You only need a laptop once, during setup.

Is there a subscription?

No. There's nothing to pay and nothing to renew.

Does it need the internet?

No. Everything runs on your home network. Your phone needs to be on your home Wi-Fi to control the oven.

Can I add another phone or tablet?

Yes, as many as you like. Pairing a new device happens entirely on your home network, with no account and no cloud.

Will I lose my recipes?

Cook programs and controls you use from the phone keep working locally. Anything that only ever lived on June's servers can't be brought back by this project.

What happens after a factory reset?

That depends on how you set it up. With the standard setup, a factory reset removes Project July and the oven goes back to how it came. With the reset-proof setup, the oven keeps its local control after a reset. You might need to pair your phone again.

Can I undo it?

Yes. With the standard setup, a factory reset removes everything Project July added and puts the oven back the way it came. If you'd rather keep local control no matter what, choose the reset-proof setup. Then a factory reset won't take your local control away.

Does it work with Home Assistant?

Yes. The June Oven integration for Home Assistant adds the oven with its status, temperature and cook controls, plus a dashboard card styled after the oven's own glass screen. Install it through HACS. The live camera is also available as a standard MJPEG stream.

Is this made by June?

No. Project July is an independent community project by June owners. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by June or Weber.

Who's doing this

Built by owners, for owners

We own June ovens too. The hardware still works well, and it seemed wrong for good ovens to lose their features because a server was switched off.

Project July is a volunteer, community project. There are no sponsors and nothing to sell. If you have a question, feel free to email us. We'll help where we can.
